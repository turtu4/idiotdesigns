Shine & Sparkle

Light Studies with Children

Shine & Sparkle is a collaborative project led by Julia Mateo Martinez, Vaishanavi Mungekar, and me aimed at transforming public spaces into inclusive and engaging environments for children. Featured at the Summer Festival in Frihamnen, Gothenburg, to celebrate the city’s 400-year jubilee, the project focused on revitalizing overlooked and underutilized spaces—particularly dark or neglected areas—into vibrant, playful environments.

In partnership with Prototyping Gothenburg and FORMAS, we’ve chosen a unique spot under the bridge in Frihamnen, using its vast walls and proximity to water to integrate light, reflection, and movement into a playful experience. Working with children from IES Krokslätt, we co-design an interactive installation that reflects their creativity.

The playland, made from recycled materials, promotes sustainability while offering children a space for exploration and socialization. Our goal is to create a dynamic, sensory-rich environment that invites children to engage with their surroundings in a new, playful way.
