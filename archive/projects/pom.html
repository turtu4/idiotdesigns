Pomana

DIY Ceremony

Pomana is a case study exploring the Romanian neo-tradition of Pomana de Viu, where individuals perform a ritual for the deceased while still alive. Originating from concerns over the younger generation's neglect of the tradition, participants organize the ceremony themselves, offering food, gifts, and a priest's presence as though honoring the deceased. The study examines the evolution of this ritual, its role in cultural preservation, and its paradoxical nature. Pomana de Viu serves as both a form of self-purification and a means of community support, blending spiritual and material practices. The project analyzes its impact on individuals and the wider community, highlighting the complex spiritual, social, and cultural implications.
