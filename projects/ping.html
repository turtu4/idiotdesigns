Penhold Ping Pong Paddle

Yep, a Penhold Ping Pong Paddle, That's It

This project involved crafting a custom penhold ping pong paddle from scratch, using high-quality Swedish Oak wood for its durability and aesthetic appeal. The process began with carefully shaping the handle to fit the ergonomic needs of the penhold grip. The blade was meticulously cut and sanded to achieve the optimal balance of speed and control, serving as both functional and visually appealing.
