NUNU

Silly Stuff, Nothing Serious

NUNU — Silly Stuff, Nothing Serious is a Dadaist exploration of immigration and the fluidity of memory, capturing the disorienting process of reflecting on past experiences. Through fragmented language, absurd scenarios, and surreal imagery, it delves into the emotional complexity of adjusting to a new life. The narrative shifts between chaotic thoughts, cultural clashes, and moments of self-realization, illustrating how migration reshapes identity.

By incorporating humor, confusion, and poignant insights, the project challenges viewers to reconsider how we remember, reframe, and reconstruct our pasts in the face of displacement. Featuring a combination of film photography and AI-generated distortions of these images, NUNU creates a visual and conceptual blend that mirrors the fluid, fractured nature of memory, offering an immersive reflection on adaptation and the reshaping of self.
