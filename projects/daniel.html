Farväl

by Daniel Wirgård

My mother died.

She had chemotherapy and caught pneumonia, and kidney failure. They gave her antibiotics and told me she would recover. Then they would continue the treatment. She told me something else, but I didn't listen.

I wasn't at all prepared. I had not told her the words I would've told her if I knew she was leaving. In fact the last thing I said to her was blaming her for not fighting, trying to lecture her.

I found her dead and felt deceived. She was only 73 years old.

She knew I loved her, I know she loved me. But still.

Always reflect on the possibility of death.
