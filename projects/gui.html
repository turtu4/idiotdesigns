Scenography

Conflicting Set Design

The Guide, directed by Yana Deliyska, is a short film following a crime detective wrestling with dissatisfaction in his work and a persistent dream of becoming a novelist. Over time, his psyche fractures and the character splits in two: himself and his alter ego, “the guide.” Through this doubling, the film interrogates the complexities and pitfalls of extreme individualism.

As set designer, my role is to construct a minimalist and symbolic environment that tracks this internal conflict and gradual self-confrontation. The spaces remain stripped-back and carefully composed, so that small changes in objects, light, and distance between characters signal shifts in the protagonist’s mental state.

During the initial research phase, I undertook an internship at Koncopteriet, an architecture and interior design studio specialising in sustainable practices. Under the guidance of founder Stephan Hruza, I contributed to Koncepteriet’s furniture project while receiving critical input for the spatial language of the film. Filmcentrum Syd supported the production with creative feedback and essential tools needed to realise the project.
