Boredom

A State of Mind

Boredom — A State of Mind is an environmental installation built from a single strip of tape warped along the corridor walls and floor of a student housing building. From almost any position the piece reads as a glitch: a broken frame that does not align, a visual irritation in the periphery of everyday movement. But when viewed from one precise point, the distortion snaps into a perfect rectangle, a familiar "picture frame" that suddenly invites passage. Residents could walk through this optical threshold, briefly inhabiting the image instead of simply looking at it. The work probes boredom not as emptiness but as latent potential—how a dead, over-familiar corridor can be made to misbehave, offering a small but tangible shift from passive seeing to active participation.
