Friends as Tools for Play

Music that Fell from the Tree

Our project aims to create an engaging and cooperative play environment where children can discover sound and experience music as a means of communication. Together, Joséphine Perini Stefansson and I designed a music installation seamlessly integrated into the natural surroundings of the Plikta playground in Slottsskogen.

This large-scale instrument is attached to the crown of a tree, from which six wooden, water-drop-shaped shutters hang at different reachable heights. When pulled, these shutters trigger the instrument to produce specific musical notes, creating an interactive and immersive sound experience.

The installation is designed to transport users beyond their everyday surroundings, allowing them to step into a realm of music and fantasy. Rooted in the idea of “the more, the merrier,” our project encourages collaboration, as only through teamwork can children unlock the full potential of the musical experience. This piece is both an artistic and social experiment in playful sound exploration.
