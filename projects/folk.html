Murderfolk

Hopeless Noise

Murderfolk is a graphic design project focused on creating album cover art for Veive's metal noise album. The design aims to capture the raw, visceral intensity of the music, exploring themes of violence, decay, and societal grime. Inspired by the harsh sonic textures of the album, the artwork uses imagery of decay and urban desolation, with a color palette of black and baby pink to emphasize the tension and grotesque nature of the sound. The gothic typography and provocative illustrations, including a defiant figure with an amputated finger, visually reflect the album's confrontational and gruesome tone.
