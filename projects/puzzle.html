Analogue Connections

Feeling Puzzled

Analogue Connections — Feeling Puzzled is a material toy experiment made from a composite of cooked rice and shredded egg carton, pressed and dried overnight in a wooden mold. The resulting pieces carry a rough, irregular texture that resists the polished logic of industrial toy production. Their slight imperfections make the act of assembling them tactile and embodied, revealing the traces of how they were formed.

Created as a gift for a close friend, the puzzle serves as a physical metaphor for connection: each piece is complete on its own but only becomes structurally meaningful when linked with another. Rather than using narrative or symbolism, the project lets form, touch, and material behaviour express the nature of relational bonds—simple, handmade, and dependent on being held together.
