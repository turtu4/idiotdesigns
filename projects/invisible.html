Invisible Exhibitions is a design project that explores the intersection of art, rural communities, and the concept of space. Realized in the village of Fröseke, the project challenges the traditional notion that artistic development is confined to urban centers. By merging the work of local artists with that of external creatives, the exhibition invites the audience to explore and discover art in a rural setting. The exhibition is not scheduled or announced, creating a dynamic where the space becomes an exhibition only when perceived, lived, and conceived by the visitors, transforming ordinary surroundings into cultural experiences.
