Samhållighet

Biodegradable Bandages — by Andrea

Samhållighet is the Swedish term that encapsulates the essence of "sustainability" and "cohesiveness."

How can we innovate the design and material composition of biodegradable bandages to not only enhance their effectiveness in wound care but also minimise environmental impact — contributing to a model for environmentally conscious healthcare solutions that can be rapidly implemented and scaled up?

In response to the escalating demand for sustainable healthcare solutions, this material exploration project aims to revolutionise wound care by designing biodegradable bandages, boldly exploring the potential of biodegradable materials as a replacement for prevalent petroleum-based industrial plastics. Can this shift be the antidote to the environmental emergency, and when might we witness its widespread adoption?

This endeavour represents a forward-looking fusion of product design practice and speculative design logic. Through the synthesis and application of novel plastics, the project ponders these pressing questions while proposing practices for a more sustainable future.
