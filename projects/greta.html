Journey

Self Portrait — by Greta

This artwork means a lot to me. It was the breaking point of my own personal art journey.

During my teenage years, studying art in school, I had become rigid. I lost the freedom of exploration I had as a child — the thing that always brought me the most joy when creating. For some years I barely even created anything.

This particular piece came from a moment of a lot of strong emotions about self identity, that needed to be let out in some way. I just sat down, forgot all my usual worries of "will it look good to other people" or "is this the right way to do it," and just drew.

The lines are messy. I used as many materials as I felt like to make it look right — not to someone else, but just to me. I just felt, and did. No thinking.

The artstyle I used for these self portraits was so different from my usual attempts at portraits, that I was never quite happy with. At the same time I realised that it's okay to change my mind.
