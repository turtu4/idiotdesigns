Mental Voyage, Leva & Under Stenen — by Greta

Mental Voyage / Leva — watch Mental Voyage · watch Leva

This recording project was a learning experience. We invited Erik Bodin, the drummer, to studio Klangverket — he had not heard the song before, and we wanted to record the core band live in the studio. After setting up, we sat together to play through the song, and just 20–30 minutes later, the groove was there. Recording it was quite easy; a few takes later we had the foundations. I find recording live in the studio like that very rewarding and fun — you learn a lot about dynamics and communication.

Under Stenen — watch Under Stenen

I met Nikolaj Flor Rotne at a seminar for project management in Denmark. After a few days of classes and workshops, we had met many new friends and started exchanging contact info. Nikolaj asked me if he could interview me over a video call for an hour, and after that, he would write a poem from our conversation, send it to me, and suggested that I write a song and melody to the poem.

A few weeks after the chat, Nikolaj sent me the poem and I started reading, letting the words sink in. The song arrived, and a date was set to meet up and record it — both the music and filming it. It was a different kind of experience, and we are both happy with the outcome.
